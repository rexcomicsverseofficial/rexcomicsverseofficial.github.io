Our Story

About Rex Comicsverse

The Universe

Rex Comicsverse was born from a simple belief: India deserves its own cinematic superhero universe. One that draws from the deepest wells of Indian mythology, history, and spirit — and presents it to the world with world-class artistry.

The Indian is our first hero — a patriot, a warrior, a protector — but he will not be the last. The universe is vast. The stories are waiting.

We are building this platform episode by episode, reader by reader, with your support.
